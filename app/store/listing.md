## English (en-US)

**Title:** Simit Seagull

**App Store subtitle:** Snatch simits over the ferries

**Short description (Play):** Glide over Istanbul's ferries and snatch simits! A one-finger 3D flying game.

**Promotional text (App Store):** Nine cities from Istanbul to Mersin, dozens of outfits and a new reward every day. Glide with one finger, grab the simits and top the weekly board!

**Keywords (App Store):** seagull,bird,flying,istanbul,ferry,bosphorus,arcade,endless,runner,one finger,turkey,bagel,casual

**Full description:**

Become a seagull over the Bosphorus! Glide between ferries, waterside mansions and bridges, snatch the simits (Turkish sesame bagels) right out of passengers' hands, and dodge chimneys and kites.

🕊️ ONE-FINGER PLAY
Slide your finger left and right and the gull follows. Seconds to learn, weeks to master.

🗺️ NINE CITIES
Start on the Bosphorus; reach the Black Sea and İzmir opens. Then Antalya, Muğla, Çanakkale, Trabzon, Samsun, Rize and Mersin. Every city has its own piers, hazards and views, and each new one is harder than the last and worth more points.

🎩 OUTFITS
Dress up your gull with the simits you collect: hats, glasses, scarves, medals... Every piece has a perk that matters in flight. Complete a three-piece set for bonus points.

🎁 SOMETHING NEW EVERY DAY
Daily reward streak, a daily flight, missions and a weekly leaderboard. The week's best get a prize.

🏆 LEADERBOARD
Race gulls from around the world and challenge your friends.

✨ FEATURES
• No ads, no real-money purchases
• Plays offline (the leaderboard needs a connection)
• 20 languages
• Transfer code to move your save to another device
• No e-mail, location or personal details asked

Grab your simit! 🥯
